Quick answers

Questions students ask first

Is there negative marking?

Classes 5, 6 and 7 have none, so attempt every question. In Classes 8, 9 and 10 a correct answer earns 4 marks and a wrong answer costs 1 mark.

How long is the paper?

It runs 2 hours (120 minutes) with 80 multiple-choice questions and a maximum of 320 marks.

Offline or online?

The offline pen-and-paper test is on 25 October 2026 across 400+ cities. Online CBT from home is available on 25, 28 and 31 October.

Which syllabus is tested?

Foundational NCERT concepts from your current class and earlier topics. Part 3 lists the Class 8 and Class 9 topics. For other classes, check the official syllabus on allen.in/tallentex.

Should I guess when I am unsure?

With four options and a 1-mark penalty, a blind guess averages about +0.25 marks, so the break-even chance is 20%. The results swing a lot, though. Guess after you have ruled out at least one option, and use the guess calculator in Part 2 to see the numbers.

What happens after the exam?

Results arrive in November 2026 with your All-India Rank, CSI and scholarship entitlement. Top national rankers are invited to the Success Power Session in Kota.
